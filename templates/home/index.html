Why Download Our App?

We've redesigned the pharmacy experience. Here's how we make your life healthier and easier.

Snap & Order

Don't struggle with reading doctors' handwriting. Simply take a photo of your prescription, upload it, and our pharmacists will handle the deciphering and packing for you.

  • Instant verification by pharmacists
  • Secure medical record storage

Quick Order

Running low on your daily essentials? Re-order your past medicines in seconds with our single-click Quick Order feature. No need to search again.

  • One-tap reordering
  • Smart history tracking

Nearest Branch

Need to visit us in person? Find the Mahmood Pharmacy closest to your location instantly. We provide integrated Google Maps directions with a single tap.

  • Real-time distance calculation
  • One-tap navigation

Real-Time Tracking

Stay in the loop. Track your medicine from the moment we confirm it until it arrives at your doorstep. No more guessing games.

  • Live status updates
  • Delivery estimates
